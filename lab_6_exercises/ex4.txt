              avg_wt   avg_tat   avg_rt
FCFS           8.000    12.286    8.000
SJF            4.857     9.143    4.857
RR (q = 2)     9.143    13.429    3.429

 - Round Robin has the best response time, every process gets the cpu soon after it arrives (P3 starts after 1 s instead of 21 s with SJF). That is why RR is used for interactive systems
 - Round Robin has the WORST average waiting/turnaround time here, long jobs are cut into pieces and all of them finish late (P1, P6 finish at 29 and 31), SJF is still the best for the averages
 - Each preemption is a context switch (SIGTSTP + SIGCONT here); in a real OS it costs time, so a very small quantum wastes cpu, while a very large quantum turns Round Robin into FCFS. With q >= 8 Round Robin gives exactly the FCFS results
 - Rounf Robin never starves anybody: every process in the queue gets a quantum each round
